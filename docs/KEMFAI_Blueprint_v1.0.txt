KEMFAIProduct Blueprint v1.0
Kemet + Finance + AIQuickBooks simplicity × Odoo business power × Gulf & Egypt localization
Product objective. Launch a sellable accounting and financial-management SaaS on Odoo Community, with a clean KEMFAI module layer, strong CFO controls, and an architecture that can scale from SMEs to multi-entity groups.
1. Product Principles
Use Odoo Community as the technical foundation; keep KEMFAI functionality in kemfai_* modules and avoid direct core modification.
Best-of-both design: preserve QuickBooks-style simplicity while using Odoo-strength workflows where they add material control.
One posted ledger is the source of truth for financial statements, subledgers, projects, budgets and analytics.
Projects are first-class financial objects; Tracking Dimensions handle branch, department, cost center, salesperson and custom tags.
AI may detect, suggest and explain; posting remains deterministic and permission-controlled.
Country localization is modular: Accounting Core → Tax Engine → Country Pack → Government Connector.
Design for fast onboarding: Excel/CSV, QuickBooks and Odoo migration paths with reconciliation controls.

Localization Foundation: Tax engine and country-pack framework. Initial commercial focus: UAE, KSA and Egypt packs; Bahrain/Qatar follow without changing core.
AI V1: Ask KEMFAI over structured finance data; report/variance/cash commentary; smart bank matching; categorization suggestions; duplicate-bill detection; source-linked answers.

Advanced custom reporting and management-pack automation.
Mobile-first workflows and broader external integrations.
Controlled autonomous workflows only where accounting and approval guardrails remain deterministic.

8. P0 Definition of Done
A real trading/service/project company can be configured without code changes.
Opening balances and open subledgers reconcile to the GL.
Purchase → inventory/service cost → sale → VAT/tax → collection/payment → bank reconciliation works end-to-end.
P&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow and Trial Balance reconcile to the same posted ledger.
AR/AP statements and aging reconcile to control accounts.
Project profitability and Budget/Actual/Committed/Forecast are traceable to source transactions.
Period close, permissions, approvals and audit trail prevent silent accounting changes.
Data can be exported to Excel/PDF and migrated through controlled templates.
AI answers expose source calculations/transactions and never invent or directly post accounting entries.

10. Product Guardrails
No direct Odoo core edits; extension through KEMFAI modules.
No proprietary QuickBooks/Odoo Enterprise code copying; reproduce desired capabilities through original implementation.
No manufacturing/CRM/website/helpdesk scope in the initial accounting product.
No AI-generated ledger balances: financial numbers come from deterministic queries/calculations.
No feature enters P0 unless it supports a sellable accounting close, localization requirement, migration, or a core KEMFAI differentiator.
