1. Product Definition
KEMFAI is a multi-tenant cloud financial platform designed to combine the ease of modern small-business accounting with ERP-grade relational depth, financial control, localization and AI-assisted analysis.
Core formula: QuickBooks simplicity + Odoo business architecture and 360° connectivity + KEMFAI financial control and intelligence.
Primary launch markets: UAE, Saudi Arabia and Egypt; Bahrain and Qatar follow.
Odoo Community is the business/accounting engine. The customer must never be exposed to the native Odoo UI.
KEMFAI must be usable by a small-business owner while scaling to CFO, accounting and multi-entity workflows.
The product is SaaS: public website, pricing, signup, subscription, tenant provisioning, onboarding, accounting application and KEMFAI super-admin control center.

7. Accounting Core & Chart of Accounts
7.1 Lean COA + Parent Accounts + Dimensions
The Chart of Accounts must remain compact. Parent accounts are reporting/aggregation nodes; posting occurs only to posting accounts.
Example hierarchy: Operating Expenses [Parent] -> Occupancy [Parent] -> Rent Expense [Posting].
Do not create separate rent accounts for Dubai, Riyadh, warehouse, sales, etc. Those attributes belong to dimensions.
GL line data model concept: Account + Debit/Credit + Entity + Branch + Department + Cost Center + Project + Salesperson + Custom Dimensions.
Dimension engine: Entity, Branch/Location, Department, Cost Center, Project, Salesperson and configurable custom dimensions/tags.
Dimension validation rules may be account- or transaction-type-specific. Example: Project Cost requires Project and Cost Center.
Reports and budgets use the same dimensions. 'Analyze by' must support dimension pivots without creating new accounts.
7.2 Core GL
Journals, automatic double-entry, manual JE, recurring JE, opening balances, accruals, prepayments, fixed assets, depreciation, multi-currency GL, realized/unrealized FX, reversing entries and audit trail.
Month-end states: Open -> Soft Close -> Finance Review -> Hard Close. Reopening requires authorization and logging.
Business transactions generate accounting entries automatically; authorized accountants can drill into the accounting impact.
8. Sales & Accounts Receivable
Customer, Estimate/Quotation, optional Sales Order, Quote -> SO -> Invoice, direct invoice, progress/partial invoices, deposits/advances, recurring invoices, credit notes/refunds, discounts and payment terms.
Partial collections and one payment allocated to multiple invoices.
Customer Statement, AR Aging, reminders, credit limits, pricelists and multi-currency.
Collection Schedule supports milestone percentages/dates and feeds Expected Cash Flow.
Invoice composer defaults to Customer, Invoice Date, Due Date, Currency, optional Project, line items, tax and totals. Dimensions/accounting details sit under More Details.
Post screen shows AR, Revenue and VAT financial impact plus smart links to SO/Project/Delivery/Payment/Journal/Documents.
9. Purchases & Accounts Payable
Vendor, Direct Expense, Vendor Bill, RFQ, PO, PO -> Receipt -> Bill, direct bill, vendor advances, partial payments, credits/refunds and recurring bills.
3-way match: PO <-> Receipt <-> Bill; exception handling requires explicit warning/approval.
Vendor Payment Schedule supports advance/pre-shipment/receipt milestones and feeds treasury forecast.
PO confirmation creates committed cost against project/budget where applicable.
Vendor 360° mirrors Customer 360° with AP context.
10. Banking, Treasury & Reconciliation
Multiple bank accounts/currencies; CSV/Excel/OFX import at launch; bank APIs later.
Reconciliation: bank transaction vs KEMFAI records with suggested match, confidence, Match/Create/Split/Transfer/Ignore and undo/history.
Bank rules for recurring charges and transaction categorization.
Split transaction across accounts/projects/cost centers/dimensions.
PDC/cheque lifecycle: customer received/in hand/deposited/cleared/bounced; vendor issued/future dated/presented/cleared/cancelled.
Treasury formula: Current Cash + Expected Collections + Customer Cheques - Bills Due - Vendor Cheques - Scheduled PO Payments.
P1: 13-week cash forecast.
11. Projects & Budgeting
11.1 Project Financial Control
Project is a first-class object, never only a tag.
Project flow: Sales -> Cost -> PO -> Bill -> Expenses -> Collections -> Profit -> Budget -> Commitments.
Overview KPIs: Contract Value, Invoiced, Collected, Outstanding, Revenue, Actual Cost, Committed Cost, Forecast Final Cost, Current GP and Forecast Final GP.
Tabs: Overview, Revenue, Costs, Budget, Collections, Activity, Documents.
Every project number drills to transactions and sources.
11.2 Budget Engine
Core columns: Budget | Actual | Committed | Forecast | Variance.
Annual/monthly; P&L/BS; by Account, Project and Dimensions.
Versions: Original, Revised, Forecast 1, Forecast 2; approvals and locking.
Excel import/export and copy previous year.
Committed cost derives from open/confirmed procurement commitments, not only posted actuals.
12. Inventory & Costing
Products/services, SKU/barcode, On Hand/Available/Reserved, multi-warehouse/location, receipts, deliveries, internal transfers, adjustments and reorder.
Automatic COGS, inventory valuation, Average/FIFO/Standard cost, optional lot/serial.
Landed Cost allocation by value, quantity, weight or volume.
Inventory aging/movement and project/dimension allocation.
Manufacturing/MRP is not P0.
13. Reporting & CFO Intelligence
Financial Statements: P&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, Trial Balance, General Ledger.
Management: Budget vs Actual, Actual + Committed vs Budget, Forecast vs Budget, Project Profitability, Sales/Margin and 13-week cash.
AR/AP: Aging, Customer/Vendor Statements and collection/payment forecasts.
Compliance/operations: VAT, Bank Reconciliation, Inventory Valuation/Aging.
One Financial Report Engine: Period, Company, Compare, Dimensions, Accounting Basis and Currency.
Comparisons: Budget, Last Year, Previous Period and Forecast.
Balance Sheet and P&L numbers drill to accounts -> dimensions/entities -> transactions -> source records.
Monthly Management Pack: selected reports to PDF/Excel/share; AI executive commentary later.
14. Expenses, Assets, Payroll Financial Layer & Close
Expenses: direct expense, employee claims, petty cash wallet, employee advance, corporate cards, receipts, split allocation, VAT, approvals and reimbursements.
Assets P1: asset register/categories, purchase-to-asset, depreciation, transfers, disposal and gain/loss. IFRS 16 later.
Payroll financial layer P1: employee master, salary/allowance/deduction, bonus/commission, advances/loans, reimbursement, EOS/leave accounting, payroll journal, allocation and payment file. Do not build full HR in V1.
Close Workspace: Bank, AR, AP, Inventory, Accruals, Prepayments, Depreciation, FX, VAT, Intercompany and Period Lock with owners/status.
15. Approvals, Controls, Documents & Audit
Central Approval Engine shared by modules; role/amount/company/department/project rules, multi-level approval, maker-checker, segregation of duties, delegation and rejection reasons.
Controls include duplicate invoice, over budget, credit limit, bill > PO, missing documents and post-close edits.
Document Engine links PDF/Excel/image evidence to Invoice, Bill, PO, Payment, JE, Project and Asset; preview, category, search, version history and missing-document alerts.
Audit path example: Payment -> Bill -> PO -> Receipt/GRN -> Quote -> Approval -> Bank Transaction.
Audit Center highlights manual journals, post-close entries, reopened periods, vendor bank changes, overrides and missing evidence.
16. Multi-company, Intercompany & Consolidation
Multiple legal entities, base currencies, COAs, fiscal years and tax setups with user access by company.
Group reporting currency and FX translation.
Intercompany auto-counterpart and matching differences.
Consolidation flow: Local TB -> Mapping -> Currency Translation -> Adjustments -> Eliminations -> Consolidated FS.
Basic multi-company/intercompany/consolidation is P1; advanced consolidation and minority interest are later.
17. Localization Architecture
Architecture: Accounting Core -> Tax Engine -> Country Pack -> Government Connector.
Country pack contains tax codes, tax rules, invoice requirements, VAT return, credit/debit notes, tax reports and e-invoice mapping.
Tax rates and regulatory rules must not be hard-coded into accounting core.
Priority: UAE, KSA, Egypt at launch foundation; Bahrain and Qatar follow.
Government connector layer supports UAE structured e-invoicing, KSA ZATCA and Egypt ETA as required by rollout.
18. AI Layer
Ask KEMFAI natural-language financial questions in Arabic and English.
V1: report commentary, variance explanation, cash-flow explanation, bank matching suggestions, categorization suggestions and duplicate-bill detection.
AI response contract: Answer -> Calculation -> Source Transactions. The user must be able to inspect evidence.
Posting contract: Detect -> Suggest -> Explain -> Authorized User Approves -> Post.
AI must not autonomously create/post final accounting entries without controlled authorization.
19. SaaS Subscription & KEMFAI Super Admin
Subscription lifecycle: Trial -> Subscribe -> Active -> Renew -> Upgrade/Downgrade -> Payment Failed -> Grace -> Suspended -> Reactivate -> Cancel.
Expired/cancelled subscription must not delete customer data.
Feature Entitlements Engine: Plan -> Features -> Limits. Never hard-code logic such as if plan == Starter.
Admin can create/edit plans, monthly/annual prices, feature limits, trials, coupons, complimentary/custom pricing and publish/unpublish plans.
Super Admin: Customers, Tenants, Plans, Pricing, Features, Subscriptions, Payments, Coupons, Usage, Revenue/MRR, Support, Audit Log and System Health.
Downgrade validates active usage against target limits before completion.

21. Technical Architecture
Launch architecture is a modular SaaS monolith, not microservices.
Layer
Decision
Frontend
Next.js / React, KEMFAI UI, Carbon-based component layer.
API/BFF
KEMFAI-controlled API boundary between frontend and Odoo; native Odoo UI is not customer-facing.
Business Engine
Odoo Community with isolated kemfai_* modules; never modify Odoo core directly.
Database
PostgreSQL.
Background Work
Job/queue mechanism for imports, reports, notifications, connectors and AI tasks.
External Services
Identity/Auth, Subscription/Billing, File Storage, Email/Notifications, AI, Government connectors.
Tenancy
Database-per-tenant. Every customer/tenant receives a separate PostgreSQL/Odoo database from launch; tenant routing/provisioning is controlled by KEMFAI.
Reuse Odoo foundations for users/companies/contacts/products, ORM/security, accounting objects/currencies/journals, sales/purchase and inventory.
Extend/customize accounting UX and controls, bank/treasury, reporting, projects, budgets, dimensions, approvals, intercompany/consolidation, localization and AI.
Use Odoo Community/LGPL-compatible code only; do not copy proprietary Enterprise modules.
22. Data & Domain Guardrails
Use immutable IDs and explicit tenant/company ownership on domain records.
Posted accounting entries are append/correct/reverse oriented; never rely on destructive edits.
All financial source relationships must be explicit, queryable and auditable.
Dimensions are normalized business attributes, not encoded into account names.
Money values require currency, precision and base-currency handling.
Dates/times require company/user timezone semantics and audit timestamps.
Files/evidence must use secure object storage references, not database blobs where avoidable.
Critical workflows should be idempotent: payment callbacks, posting, provisioning, connector ingestion and background jobs.

25. Agent Implementation Contract
Any implementation agent must treat this document as the baseline product contract.
Do not replace agreed UX with native Odoo screens.
Do not change navigation, accounting model, parent/dimension philosophy or 360° relationship rules without an explicit product decision.
Implement vertical slices end-to-end: UI -> API/BFF -> domain/service -> Odoo/posting -> audit -> tests.
Every story must define permissions, tenant/company scope, audit events, empty/error/loading states and accessibility.
Every financial feature must define posting behavior, reversals, multi-currency behavior and dimension propagation where applicable.
Every KPI/report must document calculation logic and drill-down target.
No plan, tax, approval or localization rule may be hard-coded when configuration is appropriate.
Automated tests must cover accounting invariants: debits=credits, tenant isolation, authorization, idempotency and source linkage.
Use feature flags for incomplete modules. Do not expose partially implemented financial workflows to production users.
If the specification is ambiguous on a material accounting/product decision, stop and raise a decision request rather than inventing behavior.
26. Definition of Done - P0
A new customer can self-register, subscribe/trial, provision a workspace and configure a company.
User can complete customer-to-cash and vendor-to-payment workflows with correct accounting entries.
Bank transactions can be imported, matched and reconciled with undo/history.
Projects expose revenue, cost, committed cost, collections, budget, forecast and profitability.
COA supports parent/reporting accounts and posting accounts; dimensions prevent account explosion.
P&L, BS, Cash Flow, TB, GL, AR/AP Aging, Project P&L and Budget vs Actual are available and drillable.
Dashboard is role-based, configurable and actionable.
Every key object supports 360° navigation and evidence/audit history.
Tenant isolation, roles/permissions, audit logs, posting controls and period close rules pass automated tests.
Customer-facing UI contains no native Odoo UI dependency.
